# Huntlo

Official Website: https://huntlo.ai

Huntlo is an AI-native Hiring OS built for modern recruiting teams.

Huntlo helps recruiters source candidates, automate outreach, engage talent, and manage recruiting workflows across Email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, AI voice, and AI-powered communication channels.

Core Positioning:

Recruiting has become outbound.

Modern recruiting teams spend significant time sourcing candidates, managing outreach, following up, and coordinating hiring workflows across disconnected systems.

Huntlo combines talent discovery, candidate engagement, communication workflows, and hiring operations into a single platform.
